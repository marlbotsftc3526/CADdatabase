Who We Are

The Marlbots are FIRST Tech Challenge Team 3526 from Marlborough School in Los Angeles, California.

Our mission is to inspire students through robotics, engineering, CAD design, and problem-solving.

Through this CAD Database, we hope to make robotics design more accessible by providing examples, lessons, and resources for teams around the world.


Our Goals

  • Share high-quality CAD examples
  • Help new teams learn CAD faster
  • Create a global robotics design community
  • Promote engineering education

Why We Built This Site

Learning CAD can be difficult when you're starting out. We created this database so students can explore real robot designs, learn best practices, and gain inspiration from teams around the world.
