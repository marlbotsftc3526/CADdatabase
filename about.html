About the Marlbots

Hi! We are the Marlbots 3526, a FIRST Tech Challenge Team from Marlborough School in Los Angeles, California. Since our founding in 2009, we have combined engineering, creativity, and teamwork to design highly competitive robots while inspiring the next generation of innovators through our distinct community outreach initatives. We have had the opportunity to compete at the highest levels of FTC, most recently winning 1st Inspire at the NE Premier Event, while sharing our passion for STEM with people from around the world.

As a team, we love to collaborate. All of our team members work to design, CAD, and program our robot each year. In addition, we spearhead community service, learning leadership, communication, and problem-solving skills. We believe that the best solutions come from diverse perspectives and a willingness to learn from both successes and failures.

The CAD Database was created as an extension of that mission. Throughout our FTC journey, we have benefited from the generosity of teams that openly shared their designs, lessons, and experiences. We built this platform to give back to the robotics community by providing CAD examples, educational resources, and design inspiration for students around the world. Whether you're opening CAD software for the first time or refining a championship- level robot, we hope this database helps you learn, create, and grow.

Above all, we strive to embody the values of FIRST: innovation, gracious professionalism, and a commitment to making STEM more accessible for everyone. We are proud to be part of a global robotics community that learns together, shares knowledge, and pushes the boundaries of what students can achieve. We hope you enjoy!

💜 Want to Chat Robots?

We're always excited to connect with fellow builders, programmers, designers, mentors, and robotics enthusiasts. If you've got a cool CAD project to share, a question about the database, or just want to say hi, don't be a stranger—we'd love to hear from you!
